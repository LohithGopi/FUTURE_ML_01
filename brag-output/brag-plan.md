# Brag Plan: FUTURE_ML_01 (Cinematic Trailer Edition)

## What is this app?
An enterprise-grade Machine Learning sales and demand forecasting engine that transforms years of chaotic transactional records into predictive foresight, complete with dynamic 90% service-level safety stock buffers to eliminate stockouts.

## The angle
Trailer-scale cinematic presentation: treating commercial inventory forecasting like a high-stakes enterprise operation. Dramatic typography, wide anamorphic frames, deep glowing neon accents, and slow cinematic scale push-ins.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A sweeping, dramatic statement:
"EVERY BUSINESS RUNS ON INVENTORY.
MOST RUN ON GUESSWORK."
... fading into: "UNTIL NOW."

## Key moments (the middle)
- **1,458 Days of Temporal Intel:** 9,994 orders, 35 engineered lag features, and continuous cyclical mappings with strict zero-leakage guarantee.
- **The Out-of-Time Benchmark Showdown:** Strict 90-day forward testing revealing Random Forest crushing baseline persistence with 67.06% wMAPE.
- **The 30-Day Forward Horizon:** Glowing $72,141.44 forecast with an animated, shaded 90% safety stock envelope.

## Outro / punchline
"FUTURE_ML_01.
DEMAND INTELLIGENCE FOR THE ENTERPRISE.
Built for Future Interns."
