# Brag Plan: FUTURE_ML_01 (Sales & Demand Forecasting Engine)

## What is this app?
An automated, production-grade Machine Learning demand forecasting pipeline that transforms raw retail transactions into continuous time-series intelligence, predicting 30-day forward demand and generating dynamic 90% service-level safety stock buffers to eliminate stockouts.

## The angle
In retail, guessing inventory kills margins. This isn't a toy notebook—it's a complete, zero-leakage ML pipeline benchmarked against out-of-time holdouts and delivering real commercial metrics (wMAPE, safety stock bands, and $72K forward revenue planning).

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
High-contrast bold kinetic typography slamming in: "STOP GUESSING INVENTORY." paired with an immediate real data claim: "9,994 retail transactions. 1,458 continuous trading days."

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Zero-Leakage Feature Engine:** 35 engineered temporal features (autoregressive lags $t-1$ to $t-30$, cyclical sine/cosine calendar encodings, and rolling EMA).
- **Out-of-Time Model Leaderboard:** A live-styled benchmark card comparing Baseline (91.19% error) against Random Forest (🏆 67.06% wMAPE, $2,064 MAE).
- **Forward Horizon & Safety Stock:** Real forecast projection of $72,141.44 with a shaded 90% service-level safety stock band.

## Outro / punchline
"FUTURE_ML_01: Production-ready demand intelligence. Built for Future Interns."

## User flow worth showing
1. **Raw Transaction Ingestion:** 9,994 orders parsed and cleaned chronologically.
2. **Feature & Resampling Engine:** Continuous 1,458-day calendar with zero-leakage rolling statistics.
3. **Forecasting & Safety Buffer Delivery:** 30-day projection with automated inventory buffer alerts.
